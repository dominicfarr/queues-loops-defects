Delivery Flow Prototype

Tickets move through the process in Faster and More Predictable. Press a button and the page simulates thousands of tickets again.

The question: with loops off, can this reproduce the article's totals of 10, 17 and 25 days, and its check of 24 for a developer twice as fast and 27 for one twice as slow? With loops on, does a 2-day ticket plausibly take months, and under what conditions? Does this way of modelling behave sensibly enough to specify?

"Months" here means more than 42 working days, which is two calendar months. Elapsed time only: labour cost and cost of delay are not modelled here.
